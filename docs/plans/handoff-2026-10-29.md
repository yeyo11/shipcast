# Handoff, 2026-10-29: the refactor closed, what comes next

Where Shipcast stands at the end of the run of [goal-refactor.md](goal-refactor.md), for whoever
continues. It follows [handoff-2026-10-28.md](handoff-2026-10-28.md). The owner stopped the
measurement on 2026-10-29; the before/after numbers and their caveats are in
[refactor-report.md](refactor-report.md).

## Production

`apps-1` runs `b72cf0b`, the whole refactor, deployed by CI at 16:13 UTC on 2026-10-29 (a push to
`main` deploys: CI, the image, the deploy); the deploy of `9d68d49` before it had failed. The details
are in [../server.md](../server.md#what-production-runs-2026-10-29).

- **Three processes from one image**, by `SHIPCAST_ROLE`: `web` (rolled out with docker-rollout),
  `worker` (the short, network-bound queues and the crons) and `heavy` (AI, git, Chromium, ffmpeg and
  the probe).
- **Postgres behind PgBouncer** in transaction pooling; the migrator connects past it, and since
  `3797ae7` every entry point does so and releases its lock by hand
  ([../decisions/migration-lock.md](../decisions/migration-lock.md)).
- **Two Redis**: `redis-queue` (AOF, `noeviction`) for BullMQ, locks and one-time values;
  `redis-cache` (LRU) for caches and events ([../decisions/redis.md](../decisions/redis.md)).
- **All eight flags on**: `publishing-service`, `batched-metrics`, `realtime-ui`, `redis-state`,
  `bullmq`, `secrets-table`, `ai-runner`, `github-events`. `ai-runner` was then **retired**: the AI task
  runner is the only AI path, and the name is accepted and ignored (`RETIRED` in
  `packages/runtime/src/flags.ts`).
- **Shipcast's own headless claudio** 0.11.0 (`ghcr.io/yeyo11/claudio:0.11.0-headless`) in the
  stack, with a daily spend cap, reached at `http://claudio:8797` ([../claudio.md](../claudio.md)).
- **The public webhook host**, `shipcast-hooks.josegarrido.dev`, forwards only
  `POST /api/github/webhook` (HMAC first, rate-limited per client); the rest of the app stays on the
  tailnet.
- **Secrets in the encrypted `secrets` table** (`SHIPCAST_SECRET_STORE=table`), the 22 keys of the
  encrypted file copied and read back.
- **The legacy AI layer and the global AI budget are removed**: `SHIPCAST_MAX_BUDGET_USD` and the
  `ai-budget-usd` setting are gone; each AI task has its own spend limit
  ([../decisions/ai-layer.md](../decisions/ai-layer.md)).
- **YouTube publishes public**: Shipcast asks for `public`, and the Sluice article's two videos, the
  first uploads, came out public with the API project unaudited. `private` is a fallback should Google
  hold an upload ([../decisions/youtube.md](../decisions/youtube.md#privacy-and-googles-audit)).
- **Fixed after the measurement window**, in production with `b72cf0b`: the cold start of a page's
  first visit after a deploy (`e40d8be`, bundling and warm-up), the freshness check's count
  (`085974f`), and the migration lock (`3797ae7`).

### The incident of 2026-10-29

A script connected through PgBouncer left the migrator's session lock on a pooled server connection,
and every process started after it waited forever; `worker` and `heavy` went unhealthy. It was
recovered with an emergency rollback (`deploy-stack`) and `pg_terminate_backend` of the idle holder,
and fixed in `3797ae7` ([../decisions/migration-lock.md](../decisions/migration-lock.md)).

## The refactor's result

In short ([refactor-report.md](refactor-report.md)): provider calls −29 % a day (target −70 %),
GitHub −54.7 % with 61.7 % of its GETs answered 304, `getLaunch` from 1,972 ms to 265 ms with no
external call, the launch page from 3.9 s to 1.6 s, an idle tab at 0 requests a minute, 0 duplicates
and 0 missed schedules. The after-window was 2 hours and held the Sluice article's publish, so these
are directions, not proofs. A channel costs 4 files and 2 registry lines.

## How work is done here

As in [handoff-2026-10-28.md](handoff-2026-10-28.md), with what changed since:

- Changes go straight to `main` until a stable version
  ([../decisions/main-until-mvp.md](../decisions/main-until-mvp.md)), and a push to `main` deploys
  production. Parallel agents work on branches of their own; the lead integrates and deploys.
- Before a deploy, no scheduled post due within 20 minutes; a `pg_dump` first, the table counts
  compared after.
- A new script or command that opens the registry passes `poolOptions(process.env)`, so its migrator
  never goes through PgBouncer.
- Verify only through Sluice's `verify`; documents only through pando's `kb_add_document`.
